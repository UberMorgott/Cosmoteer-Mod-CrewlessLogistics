Crewless Logistics
[h1]Crewless Logistics[/h1]
[i]Power and supplies without the constant hauling.[/i]

Build ships where cables deliver batteries and conveyors move supplies. Your crew can spend less time hauling resources and feeding powered devices.

[h2]Electricity without crew delivery[/h2]
Cables carry whole batteries from reactors and Power Capacitors straight to powered devices. Crew delivery still works alongside them.
[list]
[*][b]Electrical cable:[/b] four automatic connections; blocks crew. Green lamps glow when a connected source has a whole battery.
[*][b]Crew crossing:[/b] crew cross perpendicular to the cable at 80% speed.
[*][b]Heat/electric crossover:[/b] heat along one axis, electricity along the other.
[*]Every reactor, capacitor and powered device has an [b]Electrical Network[/b] switch. It is off by default and saved. Turn it on for each device you want connected.
[*]Reactors supply first. Capacitors supply only when no connected reactor has a whole battery; they charge from reactors after devices are supplied.
[*][b]Engine Rooms[/b] receive network power and pass batteries to the engines and thrusters they buff. Enable the room's Electrical Network and power distribution switches, plus each recipient's Electrical Network switch.
[/list]
Supports all vanilla powered devices, with optional compatibility for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3602446152]Diagonal Thruster Plus[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3541853292]Diagonal Cockpits[/url].

[h2]Conveyor logistics[/h2]
Parts are in the [b]Storage[/b] and [b]Power[/b] tabs.
[list]
[*][b]Conveyor belt:[/b] two lanes carrying any stackable resource, including ammo: bullets, missile parts, sulfur and more. Speed: 2 tiles/s. Curves automatically and accepts side-loading. Crew can walk on belts at 70% speed.
[*][b]Belt connector:[/b] a directional box pulling from rooms or belts behind and beside it, feeding the cell in front. Connect room to belt, belt to room, room to room or belt to belt. Works with cargo holds, factories and weapons. Needs no power.
[*][b]Connector filter:[/b] Auto by default: passes the only resource both sides can use, otherwise nothing. Choose Any, one resource, or None to stop it. Sources take turns by default; Back/Left/Right priority takes from that side first.
[*][b]Belt splitter (1x2):[/b] alternates items between two outputs and can merge two belts. Set input/output priorities or use a resource filter to send the chosen resource to one side.
[*][b]Belt crossing:[/b] a straight belt over a Heat Pipe or electrical cable. Items travel along one axis, heat or batteries along the other.
[*]Belt parts show damage. Items on a part drop when it is destroyed or removed.
[/list]

[h2]Required: Yet Another Mod Loader[/h2]
Conveyors run in a C# library and need [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3577650065]Yet Another Mod Loader[/url]. Without it, belts are plain floor.
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to the loader and wait for the download.
[*]Close the game. Open [b]<Steam>\steamapps\workshop\content\799600\3577650065[/b] and run [b]Installer.bat[/b].
[*]Start the game, enable Crewless Logistics in the Mods list, trust its library (DLL) when asked, and restart.
[*]After every update of this mod, trust the library again.
[/olist]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Enable the mod after installing the loader as above.
[*]Build cables from the [b]Power[/b] tab and turn on each device's [b]Electrical Network[/b] switch.
[*]Build belts and connectors from the [b]Storage[/b] tab to move supplies between rooms.
[/olist]

[h2]Multiplayer and limits[/h2]
All players need the same mod version, the same loader and the same game version.
Only whole batteries move. Devices from mods that ship DLLs or use their own energy types are not supported.
